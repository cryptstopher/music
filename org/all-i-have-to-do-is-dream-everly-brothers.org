* Introduction

The guitar chart for this song is written capo IV, using C-shaped chords. On piano there's no capo, so this sheet is transposed up a major third to the real sounding key: *E major*. Every capo-shape chord in the original chart lands cleanly on a diatonic (or secondary-dominant) chord in E major, confirming this is the correct reading of the recording.

* Key & Theory

  :PROPERTIES:
  :NOTE: Guitar original is capo IV with C-shape chords; this sheet is written in the real sounding key, E major
  :END:

The verse is the classic 1950s "doo-wop changes" loop, *I - vi - IV - V* (E - C#m - A - B), the same progression underneath "Stand By Me" and countless other late-50s ballads. The bridge moves IV - iii - ii - V (A - G#m - F#m - B), colored by two secondary-dominant 7th chords that sharpen the turnarounds.

** Tips

- LH plays the root in the bass; RH plays the full chord
- E major has four sharps (F# C# G# D#), so most of these triads naturally sit under the hand with a black key in the middle, don't be intimidated by the key signature
- *F#7 to B* (V7/V to V) is the bridge's first release: the A# in F#7 pulls up into B, lean into it
- *E7 to A* (V7/IV to IV) is the bridge's second release: the G# stays put as a common tone while D pulls down into C#, giving A its color

* Inversions for Voice Leading

The verse loops through E, C#m, A, B repeatedly at a bright tempo, smooth voice leading keeps the right hand from jumping around on every repeat.

| Chord | Position | Notes (low to high) | When to Use                                    |
|-------+----------+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| E     | Root     | E, G#, B             | Default tonic landing                          |
| E/G#  | 1st inv. | G#, B, E             | G# in bass, smooth from C#m                    |
| E/B   | 2nd inv. | B, E, G#             | B pedal, ties E to the following B chord       |
|-------+----------+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| A     | Root     | A, C#, E             | Default IV                                     |
| A/C#  | 1st inv. | C#, E, A             | C# in bass, ties from C#m or E                 |
| A/E   | 2nd inv. | E, A, C#             | E pedal, common tone with the tonic            |
|-------+----------+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| B     | Root     | B, D#, F#            | Default V                                      |
| B/D#  | 1st inv. | D#, F#, B            | D# in bass, smooth stepwise descent from E     |
| B/F#  | 2nd inv. | F#, B, D#            | F# pedal, connects from F#m                    |
|-------+----------+----------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| C#m   | Root     | C#, E, G#            | Default vi                                     |
| C#m/E | 1st inv. | E, G#, C#            | E in bass, common tone with the tonic          |
| C#m/G#| 2nd inv. | G#, C#, E            | G# pedal, smooth from E or G#m                 |

Voice-leading tip:

For the verse's *E - C#m - A - B* loop, try E in root position, C#m in root position (they share two common tones: E and G#), then A/C# (first inversion) so the bass steps up smoothly from C# to... actually let the bass simply walk E - C# - A - B, root position throughout, and focus your smoothing on the right hand: E (E G# B) to C#m (C# E G#) moves by a single common-tone shift, and A (A C# E) to B (B D# F#) is a stepwise rise in every voice.

* Performance Notes

- *Feel*: light, bouncy 12/8 doo-wop triplet feel, this is a bright, romantic ballad, not a dirge
- *Touch*: block chords under the "dream, dream, dream, dream" hook should be warm and rung out, not clipped
- *The verse*: keep the I-vi-IV-V loop steady and metronomic, it's the engine of the whole song
- *The bridge*: lean into the F#7 to B and E7 to A resolutions, these are the song's only chromatic color and deserve a little extra weight
- *Dynamics*: soften slightly on "Only trouble is, gee whiz" before opening back up on the "I'm dreamin' my life away" cadence
